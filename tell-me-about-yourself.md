## 5-Minute Version
*(Use this for longer "walk me through your background" conversations — expand each part naturally, pausing for the interviewer.)*

Start with the **headline**: Finance & Business Data Analytics at ASU, 4.0 GPA, Government of Oman Scholar, three-time Dean's List. Mention you finished first in your diploma class — it sets the tone of consistency.

Then walk the **arc**:
1. **Where it started** — early trainee roles at Tibiaan Properties (financial records, data integrity) and Msyaaf Group, plus founding Just Brew, a cold-coffee business you still run profitably. This is where you learned margins and ownership.
2. **Corporate finance at OQ** — mid-year close, consolidated statements, variance analysis, IFRS/GAAP, commercial pricing. Your first look at finance at scale.
3. **Investment banking at Sohar International (ECM)** — the experience that set your direction. Four live deals, focal point on one, valuation on 12+ companies, $150M+ in investor materials. Talk about what it taught you: that a deal is a coordination problem as much as a numbers problem.
4. **The analytics thread** — Python pipeline (Superstore) and Microsoft Fabric lakehouse (used-car pricing). Emphasize you didn't want to only model *or* only analyze data — you wanted both.
5. **Leadership** — Financial Officer ($30K+ budget, $5K+ sponsorships, big events) and Subject Area Peer Lead (promoted from tutor; leads 8 tutors, oversees a center with 3,000+ monthly visits, 50+ students mentored, two SUN Awards).

Close with **direction**: investment banking and corporate finance now, growing toward a finance leadership role that bridges global markets and the GCC — with the same careful, consistent approach that's defined your record since day one.
