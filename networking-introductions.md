# Networking Introductions

Short, natural openers for different settings. Keep them conversational — these are starting points, not scripts.

## Career Fair
"Hi, I'm Omar — I'm a Finance and Business Data Analytics student at ASU. I've interned in equity capital markets at Sohar International and in corporate finance at OQ, and I'm really interested in your firm's [analyst / corporate finance] track. Could you tell me what you look for in candidates?"

## Coffee Chat
"Thanks for taking the time. I'm a junior at ASU studying finance and data analytics — I spent last summer in ECM at an investment bank in Oman and loved the deal side. I'd love to hear how you got into [their role], and what surprised you most early on."

## Networking Event
"I'm Omar, finance student at ASU. My background is a bit of a mix — investment banking and corporate finance internships, plus data analytics work in Python and Power BI. I'm always curious how people in [their field] actually use data day to day. What are you working on right now?"

## Recruiter Call
"Hi, I'm Omar Al Adawi, a Finance and Business Data Analytics student at ASU with a 4.0 GPA. I've completed an ECM internship at Sohar International and a corporate finance internship at OQ, and I manage a $30,000+ budget for a student association. I'm targeting analyst roles in investment banking and corporate finance, and I'd welcome the chance to talk about where I might fit."

## Alumni Outreach (email or message)
"Hi [Name], I'm Omar, a current ASU finance and data analytics student (4.0 GPA, Government of Oman Scholar). I came across your path into [firm/role] and it lines up closely with where I'm hoping to go after internships in ECM and corporate finance. If you have 15 minutes sometime, I'd really value hearing about your experience. Either way, thank you for representing ASU so well."

---

**Tip:** Always close a networking conversation by asking one genuine question and offering a clear, low-friction next step (a 15-minute call, a follow-up email). People remember curiosity and respect for their time.
