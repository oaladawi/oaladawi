## Narrative
The soft skill I lean on most is **ownership** — when something is mine, it gets done right, whether anyone is checking or not. Close behind is **communication across audiences**: I can explain a concept to a struggling student, write an investor-facing presentation, and give a peer honest feedback, and I do it in two languages. Working on live deals and a real budget has also made me comfortable being **accountable for accuracy under deadline**, which is exactly the temperament finance work demands.
