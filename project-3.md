# Project 3 — Just Brew (Founder & Venture)

**Type:** Entrepreneurial venture · **Role:** Founder · **When:** August 2023 – Present · **Location:** Muscat, Oman

---

## Problem Statement
I wanted to test whether I could turn a simple idea into a business that actually makes money — not a class exercise, but real customers, real costs, and real margins.

## Objective
Launch and run a cold-coffee business that is genuinely profitable and can grow beyond a single point of sale.

## What I Did
- Founded **Just Brew**, a cold-coffee business, and handled the full picture: product, pricing, costs, and operations.
- Built the unit economics to run at a **100%+ profit margin**.
- Grew the business to **two sales points, with a third in progress**.

## Results
- Sustained profitability with a 100%+ margin on the core product
- Expansion from one to two (soon three) locations
- Still operating today

## Business Impact / Why It Matters
Just Brew is where my finance knowledge stopped being theoretical. Setting prices, managing cost of goods, and deciding when to expand taught me margin discipline and cash awareness in a way no textbook could. When I later worked on pricing and contracts at OQ, or valuation at Sohar International, I already understood the difference between revenue and profit at a gut level.

## Key Takeaways
Owning something end to end — the wins and the mistakes — is the fastest way to learn business. It also taught me persistence: keeping a venture running for two-plus years alongside a full course load and internships.

## Skills Demonstrated
Entrepreneurship · pricing & unit economics · margin management · operations · growth decisions · ownership
