# Finance & Accounting Trainee

### Organization
Tibiaan Properties — Muscat, Oman

### Position
Finance & Accounting Trainee

### Duration
June 2023 – July 2023

---

### Responsibilities
An early, hands-on training role focused on the fundamentals of financial record-keeping and data integrity in a real-estate business.

### Major Contributions
- Processed and corrected **thousands of financial records**, improving data integrity and the reliability of the company's books.

### Quantifiable Impact
- Thousands of records reviewed and corrected for accuracy

### Key Skills Developed
Financial record-keeping · data integrity · accuracy and attention to detail · accounting fundamentals

### Lessons Learned
This was one of my first real finance roles, and it taught me a lesson I still rely on: clean data is the foundation of everything that comes after it. You cannot build a model, a report, or a decision on records you do not trust. The patience this work required is the same patience I now bring to valuation and analytics.

*Note: This was a short, early-career training role. I have kept it in my portfolio because it is where my finance fundamentals started.*
