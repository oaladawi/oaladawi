## 500 Words

Omar Al Adawi is a Finance and Business Data Analytics student at the W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University, where he holds a perfect 4.00 GPA as a Government of Oman Scholarship recipient and has earned Dean's List recognition in Fall 2023, Fall 2024, and Spring 2025. Before university, he graduated first in his diploma class and received a national scholarship for elite students — an early signal of the consistency that has defined his academic record since.

What makes Omar's profile distinctive is that he has seen finance from several angles before graduating. In the summer of 2025, he worked as an Investment Banking Advisory Intern in Equity Capital Markets at Sohar International in Muscat. There he contributed to four live ECM transactions, acting as the focal point on one of them and coordinating between clients, legal advisors, and internal teams. He helped prepare information memorandums, prospectuses, and investor presentations for offerings valued at more than $150 million, and he built and refined valuation analysis — discounted cash flow, trading comparables, and precedent transactions — across more than twelve companies in GCC markets.

The summer before, as a Finance & Commercial Intern at OQ, one of Oman's largest energy groups, he supported the mid-year financial close by preparing consolidated financial statements and variance analysis with attention to IFRS and GAAP standards. He also ran rapid market analysis across dozens of sources and worked with the commercial team to evaluate contracts and pricing. His earliest exposure came through trainee roles at Tibiaan Properties and Msyaaf Group, and through founding Just Brew, a cold-coffee venture he started in 2023 and still runs profitably.

Omar pairs this finance foundation with real analytics ability. In his coursework he has built a complete Python data pipeline — using pandas, numpy, matplotlib, and seaborn — to analyze several years of retail sales data and translate the patterns into business recommendations. In a separate project he helped design an end-to-end analytics lakehouse on Microsoft Fabric, modeling tens of thousands of used-car listings with a star schema and Power BI dashboards to identify what truly drives resale value.

His leadership is concrete and measurable. As Financial Officer of the Omani Student Association at ASU, he manages an annual budget exceeding $30,000, secured over $5,000 in international sponsorships, and helped coordinate major cultural events drawing hundreds of attendees. As a Subject Area Tutor — since promoted to Peer Lead, where he leads a team of eight tutors and helps oversee a support center handling more than 3,000 student visits a month — he has mentored over fifty students across financial management, valuation, and forecasting, and was recognized twice with the ASU SUN Award for supporting student success.

Fluent in Arabic and English, Omar is comfortable moving between the GCC and the United States, between the deal table and the dataset. He is now focused on building a career in investment banking, corporate finance, and strategic finance, where his blend of valuation experience, analytical rigor, and proven ownership can have the most impact.
