# My Professional Identity

## What motivates me
I'm motivated by problems where the stakes are real and the answer has to be right — a valuation that informs a deal, a budget people are counting on, a student who finally understands. I like being trusted with something that matters and being the person who delivers it accurately.

## What industries interest me
Investment banking (especially capital markets), corporate finance, and strategic finance. I'm drawn to the deal environment — where strategy, numbers, and people meet — and to roles that let me use data, not just intuition. I have a particular interest in GCC markets, where I have already worked, and in global financial centers.

## What problems I enjoy solving
- Turning a messy situation into a clear, defensible number (valuation, budgets, forecasts).
- Taking raw data and making it tell an honest story a decision-maker can use.
- Coordination problems — getting multiple parties aligned so something actually gets executed.

## What unique value I bring
Most finance students can do one thing well — either modeling *or* analytics, either deals *or* operations. My value is the **combination**: real ECM and corporate-finance experience, genuine data skills in Python and the Microsoft stack, proven ownership of a real budget and a real business, and the bilingual, cross-cultural fluency to operate between the GCC and the US. I bring the rigor of someone who has finished first and stayed at a 4.0, with the practicality of someone who has run a P&L and made payroll-sized decisions on a student budget.

## In one line
**A finance person who can sit at the deal table and still prove the thesis with data.**
