# Financial Officer — Omani Student Association (ASU)

**Organization:** Omani Student Association, Arizona State University
**Role:** Financial Officer
**When:** August 2025 – Present

---

## Overview
I own the finances of the Omani Student Association at ASU — the budget, the accounts, the reimbursements, the sponsorships, and the reporting. When the association spends money or raises it, that runs through me.

## STAR Breakdown

**Situation**
The association runs multiple large cultural events each year for the Omani and wider ASU community, funded by an annual budget exceeding **$30,000**. That money has to be tracked precisely and stretched as far as possible.

**Task**
Manage the full budget with complete accuracy, expand the association's funding base, and help deliver events that hundreds of people attend — without overspending.

**Action**
- Managed the **$30,000+ annual budget**, overseeing accounts, reimbursements, and reporting with a standard of 100% accuracy.
- Secured **$5,000+ in international sponsorships**, actively expanding the funding base rather than just administering it.
- Helped coordinate **5+ major events with 400–600 attendees each**, finding cost efficiencies along the way.

**Result**
- ~20% increase in funding through sponsorships
- ~10% reduction in event costs while maintaining (and improving) participation
- A clean, trusted financial record for the association

## Skills Demonstrated
Budget ownership · financial reporting & accuracy · sponsorship/fundraising · event finance · stakeholder coordination · accountability
