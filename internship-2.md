# Finance & Commercial Intern

### Organization
OQ (national energy group) — Muscat, Oman

### Position
Finance & Commercial Intern

### Duration
July 2024 – August 2024

---

### Responsibilities
I supported the finance and commercial teams during the mid-year period, working on financial reporting, market analysis, and commercial evaluation. This was my first exposure to corporate finance inside a large, complex organization.

### Major Contributions
- Supported **mid-year financial closing** by helping develop consolidated financial statements and variance analysis, with attention to **IFRS / GAAP** compliance.
- Conducted rapid, real-time analysis of market data drawn from roughly **25 sources simultaneously**, delivering early business-development insights to the team.
- Worked with the commercial team to **evaluate contracts and pricing strategies**, contributing to discussions on revenue and profitability.

### Quantifiable Impact
- ~20% improvement in reporting accuracy/structure during the mid-year close I supported
- Market analysis consolidated from ~25 sources
- Contributed to pricing/contract work associated with a ~10% profitability improvement

### Key Skills Developed
Consolidated financial statements · variance analysis · IFRS/GAAP awareness · market data analysis · commercial/contract evaluation · working inside a large finance function

### Lessons Learned
OQ showed me what finance looks like at scale. A mid-year close is not a single spreadsheet — it is dozens of moving inputs that all have to reconcile. I learned how much discipline accurate reporting requires, and how commercial decisions (pricing, contracts) connect directly back to the financial statements. It gave me the corporate-finance grounding that made my later ECM work make more sense.
