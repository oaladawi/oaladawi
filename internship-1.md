# Investment Banking Advisory Intern — Equity Capital Markets

### Organization
Sohar International — Muscat, Oman

### Position
Investment Banking Advisory Intern (Equity Capital Markets / ECM)

### Duration
June 2025 – August 2025

---

### Responsibilities
I supported the ECM advisory team across live transactions, working alongside senior bankers, clients, legal advisors, and internal teams. My work covered transaction documentation, valuation analysis, and deal coordination — the day-to-day mechanics of getting an offering ready for market.

### Major Contributions
- Contributed to **four live ECM transactions**, acting as the focal point on one of them and coordinating communication between clients, legal advisors, and internal teams to keep execution on schedule.
- Assisted in drafting **Information Memorandums, prospectuses, and investor presentations** for offerings valued at **$150M+**, improving transaction readiness and the quality of investor-facing materials.
- Performed **valuation analysis — DCF, trading comparables, and precedent transactions — on 12+ companies** across GCC markets, producing insights that supported pricing discussions.

### Quantifiable Impact
- 4 live transactions supported
- $150M+ in combined offering value across materials I contributed to
- 12+ companies valued
- Improved the accuracy of deal models by ~15% through more rigorous comparable and assumption checks

### Key Skills Developed
Valuation (DCF, comps, precedent transactions) · financial modeling · transaction documentation · investor materials · deal-process coordination · attention to detail under deadline pressure

### Lessons Learned
ECM taught me that a deal is a coordination problem as much as a numbers problem. The model has to be right, but the deal only moves if every party — client, legal, internal — stays aligned. Being the focal point on a transaction, even as an intern, showed me how much value there is in simply being the person who keeps everyone informed and ahead of the next bottleneck. It is the experience that confirmed I want to build my career in investment banking.
